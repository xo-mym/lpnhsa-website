About SSLG

The Supreme Student Government (SSLG) is the highest governing and foremost co-curricular student organization in Philippine educational institutions, particularly within the Department of Education (DepEd).

Its primary function is to represent student voices, protect student rights, and bridge the gap between the studentry and school administration.
