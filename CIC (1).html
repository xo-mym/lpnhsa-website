About the Club

The CIC Club is a non-partisan, school-based youth organization recognized by secondary or tertiary educational institutions and accredited by the Office of the Ombudsman in the Philippines. The program aims to empower the youth against corruption by developing leadership skills and instilling core values like honesty and social responsibility.

The Office of the Ombudsman and CIC May Jointly Undertake Activities That Aim To:

  • Cultivate the virtues of uprightness, responsibility, honesty, respect for authority, and love of country.
  • Instill a sense of good citizenship and responsible leadership.
  • Inculcate the basic principles of human rights and civic duties.
  • Promote the integration of Corruption Prevention Education (CPE) teaching modules in the school curriculum.
