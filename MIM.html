MIM • STUDENT ORGANIZATION

Math Investigatory and Modelling

This is a student organization that serves as a home for math lovers.

Objectives

To hone students' skills in mathematical problems, solving, and calculations.

Through this club, students sharpen their critical thinking and creativity while developing a sense of comfort and determination to study mathematics in a fun and exciting way.

Club Activities

  • Investigatory: Investigating, doing experiments, and learning new things.
  • Research: Researching, writing research papers, and solving problems.
  • Mathematics: Problem solving, geometry, algebra, and critical thinking.
