BSP • STUDENT ORGANIZATION

Boy Scouts of the Philippines Club

The leading credible youth-serving and volunteer organization empowering Filipino youth through inclusive, high-quality non-formal education anchored in the ideals of Scouting for active citizenship and nation-building. It helps the youth develop values and acquire competencies to become responsible citizens and capable leaders anchored in the ideals of Scouting for active citizenship and nation-building.

Boy Scouts of the Philippines Activities

  • Orientation for Scouts: Introduce recruits to the goals, values, programs, and benefits of Scouting.
  • Saturday Training: Develop Scouts' discipline, smartness, good order, and basic Scouting skills.
  • Skill-O-Rama: Demonstrate and assess Scouts' proficiency in essential Scouting skills.
  • Friday Training: Strengthen Scouts' discipline, leadership, teamwork, and responsibility.
  • Investiture and Backyard Camping: Officially welcome recruits into Scouting and develop their camping and basic Scouting skills.
  • Board of Review: Evaluate Scouts' knowledge, skills, and readiness for rank advancement.
  • Court of Honor: Recognize Scouts' achievements and award their accomplishments.
