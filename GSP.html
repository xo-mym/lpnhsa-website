Girl Scouting is a worldwide movement that provides girls and young women, aged 4 to 21 years old, a non-formal educational program based on character development and the ideals of service. The club's mission is to help young women prepare for future responsibilities. This club offers a non-formal educational program focused on character development and service.

The club's objectives are to develop leadership skills and character among members, promote community service and public engagement, encourage personal growth, enhance responsibility, and improve teamwork.

Club Activities

  • Basic First Aid: Mastering bandaging and wound treatment during emergencies.
  • Drill Exercises: Participating in earthquake drills to learn what to do before, during, and after emergencies.
  • Leading flag ceremonies at school and assisting students.
  • Participating in tree planting, Brigada Eskwela, clean-up drives, and other community service activities.
