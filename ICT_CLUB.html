Information and Communication Technology Club

About the ICT Club

The ICT Club is a student organization that promotes digital literacy, creativity, and innovation through technology. It provides students with opportunities to develop skills in computer operations, multimedia, programming, and the responsible use of information and communication technology while encouraging teamwork, leadership, and lifelong learning.

Club Activities

  • Digital Literacy and Software Skills
  • Coding and Web Development
  • Hardware and Robotics
  • Digital Media and Content Creation
  • Cybersecurity Awareness

Missions of the ICT Club

Promote Digital Literacy
Equip members with essential computer, internet, and technology skills.
Develop Technical Skills
Provide opportunities to learn programming, graphic design, networking, robotics, cybersecurity, and multimedia.
Foster Collaboration and Leadership
Build teamwork, communication, and leadership through group activities and projects.
Promote Responsible Technology Use
Encourage ethical, safe, and respectful use of digital tools and online platforms.
Serve the School and Community
Use ICT skills to assist with school events, digital campaigns, training sessions, and community outreach.
