About the Club

The Barkada Kontra Droga (BKD) is the Department of Education's (DepEd) flagship peer-based program and student club aimed at preventing substance abuse. Institutionalized in partnership with the Dangerous Drugs Board, the club empowers students to become catalysts for positive change, promoting healthy and drug-free lifestyles.

Recent Activities or Programs

  • International Day Against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking Seminar
  • Anti-Drug Awareness Programs
  • Extracurricular Activities During School Events
  • Anti-Drug Seminar for the Month of November
