AFDT • STUDENT ORGANIZATION

Almanzians Folkloric Dance Troup

Almanzians Folkloric Dance Troup is a student organization dedicated to preserving and promoting Filipino culture through traditional folk dances. The club develops students' talents in dance, discipline, teamwork, and cultural appreciation while showcasing the rich heritage and diverse traditions of the Philippines through performances and school activities.

Activities

  • Technical workshops: Practicing fundamental steps, body posture, and regional styling.
  • Fitness conditioning: Building stamina for fast-paced routines like Tinikling or Maglalatik.
  • Rehearsals: Polishing group synchronization and complex formations.
  • Cultural mapping: Preserving authentic meanings behind occupational, religious, and courtship dances.

Mission of the Almanzians Folkloric Dance Troup

Main Mission
The main mission of the AFDT Club is to encourage young Filipinos to acknowledge our rich culture through folkloric dance while maintaining their physical wellness. It also aims to preserve and promote Filipino traditions for future generations by teaching the youth about our cultural heritage and its importance to our country.
