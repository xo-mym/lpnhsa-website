The Story of LPNHS-Almanza

A glimpse into the history and identity of Las Piñas National High School – Almanza.

HISTORY LPNHS
ALMANZA
A SCHOOL WITH A STORY

From its beginnings to the community it serves today.

Las Piñas National High School – Almanza is part of the educational community that serves learners in Las Piñas. Its story reflects the growth of the school, its students, teachers, and the community around it.

Through the years, the school has continued to provide a place where students learn, build friendships, discover their talents, and take part in activities that shape their school experience.

Las Piñas National High School – Almanza stands as a public secondary school serving learners in Almanza Uno, Las Piñas City. Its story is closely connected to the growth of the community it serves and to the continuing need for accessible public education in the area.

As the school community grew, LPNHS-Almanza became more than a place for classroom instruction. Generations of students and teachers became part of its story through academics, student organizations, leadership activities, cultural programs, sports, service, and other experiences that shaped everyday school life.

The identity of the school has also been carried forward by its students and alumni. Each school year adds new experiences to that history—from the lessons learned inside the classroom to the friendships, achievements, traditions, and memories created throughout the campus.

Today, LPNHS-Almanza continues its role as part of the local educational community, giving young people a place to learn, develop their abilities, participate in school life, and prepare for the responsibilities that come beyond high school. Its history is therefore not only about the institution itself, but also about the many people who have helped make the school what it is.
