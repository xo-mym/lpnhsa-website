ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A place to learn, grow, and belong.

Las Piñas National High School - Almanza is a public secondary school serving the community of Almanza Uno, Las Piñas City.

School life extends beyond classroom lessons through student organizations, leadership activities, cultural programs, service, and community involvement.
